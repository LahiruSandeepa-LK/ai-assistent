# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) offers investments ranging from risk-free government securities to structured
wealth plans. Rates/yields are illustrative and subject to market movement.

## Treasury Bills and Bonds
ABC Bank acts as a primary dealer, offering Government of Sri Lanka Treasury Bills (91, 182 and
364-day) and Treasury Bonds (2-15 years) in the primary and secondary markets. Illustrative yields:
T-Bills 8.50%-9.25% p.a.; T-Bonds 10.00%-11.50% p.a. These are backed by the government and regarded
as gilt-edged, risk-free instruments. Minimum investment: LKR 100,000.

## Money Market Account
A flexible account paying near-market returns on call, with interest tiered by balance; illustrative
yield 8.00% p.a. on balances above LKR 1,000,000, suitable for parking surplus funds with liquidity.

## Repos and Reverse Repos
Repurchase agreements backed by government securities for short-term placements, illustrative rate
8.75% p.a. for 7-30 day tenors.

## Millionaire Investment Plan
A goal-based recurring investment to accumulate a target sum (see Deposits, ABC-DEP-001), available
in LKR and in foreign currency for non-residents.

## Inward Investment Account (IIA)
For non-residents to invest in Sri Lankan shares, unit trusts and government securities with
repatriation of capital and returns; see Non-Resident Banking (ABC-NR-001).

## Margin Trading
A facility to invest in Colombo Stock Exchange listed shares with bank financing against an approved
portfolio, subject to margin limits and marking-to-market.

## How to Invest
Investors complete a risk profile and a securities-account mandate, place orders through the Treasury
or Investment desk, and receive confirmations and periodic statements. Government securities can be
held in the customer's CDS/scripless account.
