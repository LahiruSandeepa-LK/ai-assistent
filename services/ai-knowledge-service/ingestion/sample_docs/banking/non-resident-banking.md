# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) offers foreign-currency accounts, deposits and investments for Sri Lankans
living abroad, dual citizens, and eligible foreign nationals. Rates are illustrative and subject to
change, and account types follow Central Bank of Sri Lanka foreign-exchange regulations.

## Personal Foreign Currency Account (PFCA)
A savings/current account in USD, GBP, EUR, AUD or other approved currencies, for Sri Lankans
employed abroad, dual citizens, and residents with foreign-currency income. Funds may be credited
from inward remittances, foreign-currency cash on return, or transfers from other foreign-currency
accounts. No exchange control restriction on outward remittance of the balance. Illustrative savings
interest: 1.50% p.a. (USD).

## Non-Resident Foreign Currency (NRFC) Fixed Deposits
Fixed deposits in approved foreign currencies for non-resident Sri Lankans. Terms 1-12 months,
illustrative USD rates 4.00%-6.00% p.a. Interest and principal are freely repatriable.

## Forex Plus Foreign Currency Fixed Deposit
A promotional FC fixed deposit offering higher returns, up to an illustrative 6.50% p.a. on USD for
a 12-month term, aimed at migrant workers remitting savings home.

## Inward Investment Account (IIA)
An account through which non-residents route funds to invest in Sri Lankan shares, unit trusts,
government securities and other permitted instruments, with repatriation of capital and returns
permitted under exchange-control rules.

## Foreign Currency Home Loan
Non-residents can finance a home in Sri Lanka in a foreign currency of choice; see Home Loans
(ABC-LOAN-HOME-001).

## Eligibility and Documents
Valid passport; proof of employment or residence abroad (visa/work permit, or dual-citizenship
certificate); and proof of foreign-currency income. Accounts can be opened before departure, at a
branch, or remotely via certified document verification through an overseas agent.

## Sending Money (Remittances)
ABC Bank pays out inward remittances through RemitPlus and partners including Western Union,
MoneyGram, Ria, EzRemit and Instant Cash, across its branch network, with credit to an ABC Bank
account or cash payout on identification.
