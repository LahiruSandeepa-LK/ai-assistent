# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) offers finance leasing for vehicles and equipment, and gold-backed loans
(pawning). Rates below are illustrative and subject to change.

## Vehicle and Equipment Leasing
Finance leases for new and registered (used) motor cars, vans, SUVs, three-wheelers, commercial
vehicles, motorcycles and business machinery. Facility: up to 80% of the asset value for registered
vehicles and up to 90% for brand-new vehicles. Tenure: 1 to 7 years. Illustrative rate: 13.00% p.a.
to 15.50% p.a. (reducing balance) depending on asset type and tenure. The asset is owned by the bank
until the lease is fully settled.

## Green Leasing
Concessionary leases for electric and hybrid vehicles and for renewable-energy equipment (e.g.
rooftop solar) at an illustrative 11.50% p.a., with tenure up to 7 years.

## Super Leasing
A fast-track lease with same-day in-principle approval for pre-qualified customers and reduced
documentation, at an illustrative 14.00% p.a.

## Leasing Eligibility and Documents
Applicant aged 18-65; proof of income sufficient to cover the monthly rental (rental generally not
exceeding 40% of net income); National Identity Card; and for a vehicle, the proforma invoice (new)
or valuation and registration documents (used). A guarantor may be required.

## Gold Loans (Pawning)
Short-term loans secured against gold jewellery, valued by the bank's appraiser. Advance: up to 80%
of the assessed gold value. Illustrative interest: 1.25% per month (15.00% p.a.) on standard
pawning, with lower promotional rates for shorter periods. Tenure: up to 12 months, redeemable at
any time. The customer brings the gold articles and a National Identity Card; the loan is disbursed
immediately after appraisal. Unredeemed articles may be auctioned after the grace period following
maturity, with notice to the customer.
