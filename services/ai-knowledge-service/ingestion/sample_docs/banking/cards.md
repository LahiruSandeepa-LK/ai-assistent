# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) issues credit, debit and prepaid cards on Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay. Fees
and rates below are illustrative and subject to change.

## Credit Cards
Tiers from entry Silver/Gold to Platinum and premium Visa Signature, Visa Infinite and World
Mastercard. Illustrative annual fees: Gold LKR 4,000, Platinum LKR 10,000 (often waived on meeting a
spend threshold). Illustrative finance charge on revolving balances: 2.75% per month (around 36% p.a.);
interest-free period up to 50 days on purchases settled in full by the due date. Minimum payment: 5%
of the outstanding balance or LKR 1,000, whichever is higher.

## Credit Card Eligibility
Salaried applicants with a minimum monthly income of LKR 50,000 (higher for premium tiers), or an
existing fixed deposit for a secured card. National Identity Card, income proof and a CRIB check are
required. Supplementary cards can be issued to family members.

## Card Facilities
Easy Payment Plans (instalments at participating merchants), balance transfer, cash on card,
card-to-card transfer, automatic bill settlement, and a loyalty rewards programme. Cards support
contactless tap-to-pay, e-commerce 3-D Secure, and mobile wallets (Google Pay / Apple Pay).

## Debit Cards
Issued on a savings or current account (Classic, Business and Remittance variants). Used for ATM
withdrawals, POS and online payments drawn directly from the account. Illustrative annual fee: LKR
1,500.

## Prepaid Cards
Reloadable Travel, Fuel and Web/Spend prepaid cards not linked to a main account, useful for
budgeting, online use and travel. The Travel prepaid card can be loaded in foreign currency subject
to exchange-control limits.

## Security
Customers can freeze/unfreeze a card, set channel and spend limits, and view transactions in real
time via digital banking. Report a lost or stolen card to the 24x7 card centre for immediate blocking.
