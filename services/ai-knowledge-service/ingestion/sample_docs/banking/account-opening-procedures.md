# Overview
This document describes how ABC Bank (fictional) opens accounts and performs Know-Your-Customer
(KYC) and customer due diligence, in line with Central Bank of Sri Lanka and Financial Intelligence
Unit requirements. It is an internal procedure reference.

## Standard Account Opening
1. The customer completes the account-opening mandate and specifies the product.
2. The officer verifies identity and collects KYC documents.
3. KYC and sanctions/PEP screening are performed; the risk rating is assigned.
4. The initial deposit is collected and the account is activated.
5. The debit card, cheque book and digital-banking enrolment are issued as requested.

## KYC Documents - Resident Individual
Valid National Identity Card (or passport/driving licence); proof of residential address (a utility
bill or billing statement no older than 3 months); and, where relevant, proof of income or source of
funds for higher-risk or high-value accounts.

## KYC Documents - Non-Resident
Valid passport; evidence of employment or residence abroad (visa, work permit, or dual-citizenship
certificate); and proof of foreign-currency income. See ABC-NR-001 for account types.

## KYC Documents - Business / Company
Business registration or certificate of incorporation; Articles of Association and Form 20
(directors); board resolution authorising account opening and signatories; and KYC on directors and
beneficial owners holding 10% or more.

## Customer Due Diligence and Risk Rating
Each customer is risk-rated (low / medium / high) based on product, occupation, expected turnover and
geography. Enhanced due diligence applies to politically exposed persons, high-value cash customers
and higher-risk jurisdictions. Records are retained for at least 6 years after the relationship ends.

## Self-Onboarding (Digital)
Eligible resident individuals can open a savings account via the ABC Bank digital app using the
National Identity Card and a live selfie for biometric verification, with no branch visit; the
account is activated after automated and, where needed, manual KYC review.
