# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) publishes daily telegraphic-transfer (TT) and currency-note buying and selling
rates against the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). The figures below are illustrative examples for testing and
do not reflect live market rates.

## Illustrative Exchange Rates (per 1 unit of foreign currency, in LKR)
USD: TT buying 298.50, TT selling 308.75; notes buying 296.00, notes selling 311.00.
GBP: TT buying 378.20, TT selling 392.40.
EUR: TT buying 322.10, TT selling 335.60.
AUD: TT buying 196.40, TT selling 205.80.
JPY (per 100): TT buying 198.70, TT selling 207.30.
INR: TT buying 3.48, TT selling 3.66.

The "buying" rate is what the bank pays when converting foreign currency to LKR (e.g. an inward
remittance); the "selling" rate is what the customer pays to buy foreign currency from the bank.
TT rates apply to electronic transfers; currency-note rates apply to physical cash and carry a wider
margin.

## How Rates Are Set
Rates are derived from the interbank market plus a spread, updated at least once each business day
and more often in volatile conditions. Large transactions may be quoted a negotiated rate by the
Treasury dealing desk.

## Remittance Services
Inward remittances can be received to an ABC Bank account or for cash payout through RemitPlus,
Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, EzRemit and Instant Cash. Outward remittances for permitted purposes
(education, medical, migration allowance, trade payments) are processed subject to Central Bank of
Sri Lanka exchange-control rules and submission of the required purpose documentation.

## Forward and Spot Contracts
Corporate and treasury customers can book spot FX contracts and forward contracts to hedge future
foreign-currency receivables or payables; currency options are also available. See Business Banking
(ABC-BIZ-001).
