# Overview
ABC Bank (a fictional bank) offers savings accounts, fixed deposits and structured investment
deposits in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and selected foreign currencies. All interest rates below are
illustrative examples for testing and are subject to change; actual rates are published in the
Rates & Tariffs schedule.

## Savings Accounts
ABC Bank offers Regular Savings, Super Saver (tiered high-interest), Udara Senior Citizens',
Anagi Women's Savings, Isuru Minors' and Dotcom Teen accounts. Regular Savings pays an
illustrative 3.00% p.a. on balances up to LKR 100,000 and up to 5.00% p.a. on higher tiers,
calculated on the daily balance and credited monthly. Senior Citizens' savings pay a bonus of
1.00% p.a. above the standard rate. The minimum opening balance for Regular Savings is LKR 1,000.

## Fixed Deposits (LKR)
Standard fixed deposits are offered for terms of 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 and 36 months. Illustrative
rates: 1 month 7.50% p.a., 3 months 8.25% p.a., 6 months 8.75% p.a., 12 months 9.50% p.a.,
24 months 9.75% p.a. Interest may be paid monthly, at maturity, or on a maturity-plus-bonus
basis. The minimum deposit is LKR 5,000. Premature withdrawal is permitted with an interest
penalty of 1.00% below the rate earned for the completed period.

## Special Short-Term Fixed Deposits
ABC Bank periodically offers Special 100-Day to 500-Day fixed deposits at promotional rates,
typically 0.25%-0.50% above the standard rate for the nearest term. These are aimed at customers
seeking higher short-term returns.

## Millionaire Investment Plan
A recurring-deposit plan where the customer contributes a fixed monthly amount for a chosen term
(5-15 years) to reach a target maturity value (e.g. LKR 1,000,000). Illustrative yield is
10.00% p.a. compounded. A foreign-currency version of the plan is available for non-residents.

## Foreign Currency Deposits
Personal Foreign Currency Fixed Deposits are available in USD, GBP, EUR and AUD for eligible
customers (see Non-Resident Banking). Illustrative USD FD rates range from 4.00% p.a. (1 month)
to 6.00% p.a. (12 months). The "Forex Plus" promotional FC fixed deposit offers up to an
illustrative 6.50% p.a. on USD for 12 months.

## Eligibility and Opening
Resident individuals aged 18+ can open LKR savings and fixed deposits with a valid National
Identity Card or passport and proof of address. See the Account Opening Procedures document
(ABC-PROC-001) for the full KYC checklist.
