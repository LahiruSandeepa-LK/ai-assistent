# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) serves SMEs and corporates with working-capital finance, term lending, trade
finance, guarantees and treasury services. Rates below are illustrative and subject to change.

## Accounts and Working Capital
Business current accounts, money market accounts and call deposits. Working-capital facilities
include overdrafts, short-term loans, pledge loans and domestic factoring. Overdraft illustrative
rate: 14.00% p.a. on the utilised balance, reviewed annually, secured against stock, receivables or
property.

## Term Loans and Project Financing
Medium- and long-term loans for capital expenditure, business expansion and project financing,
including renewable-energy projects. Tenure up to 10 years; illustrative rate 13.00% p.a. (reducing
balance), secured. The bank also arranges syndicated and structured financing for larger projects.

## Trade Finance
Import services: Letters of Credit (documentary credit), release of import documents, shipping
guarantees, advance payments and import demand loans. Export services: export bills purchase/
negotiation, pre-shipment loans, documentary collection and foreign-currency cheque/draft handling.
Illustrative LC commission: 0.25% per quarter on the LC value, plus SWIFT and handling charges.

## Guarantees
Performance bonds, bank guarantees, retention-money guarantees, credit guarantees and shipping
guarantees. Illustrative guarantee commission: 1.00% per annum on the guaranteed amount, subject to
security and a counter-indemnity.

## SME and Micro Finance
Biz Loans, Anagi Business Loans (for women entrepreneurs), Diribala development and green loans,
agri-business loans, agri leasing and agri pawning. Micro customers can access Dirishakthi
microfinance loans and the Divisaru savings account. Illustrative SME loan rate: 12.50%-15.00% p.a.

## Treasury for Corporates
Spot and forward FX contracts, currency options, interest-rate derivatives, REPOs and reverse REPOs,
Treasury Bills and Bonds, Sri Lanka Development Bonds (SLDBs) and Inward Investment Accounts.

## Eligibility and Documents
Business registration / incorporation certificate, Articles and Form 20 (for companies), audited
financial statements for the last 2-3 years, bank statements, tax clearance, and details of
security offered. A credit assessment and CRIB check are performed before approval.
