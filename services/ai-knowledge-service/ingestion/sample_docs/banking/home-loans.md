# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) finances the purchase, construction, completion, renovation and refinancing of
residential property. Illustrative rates and conditions follow and are subject to change.

## Home Loan (LKR)
Purpose: purchase of a house or apartment, purchase of land plus construction, construction on own
land, renovation, or balance-transfer refinancing from another bank. Loan amount: up to 80% of the
property value (loan-to-value ratio 80%). Tenure: up to 25 years. Illustrative interest rate:
11.50% p.a. (reducing balance), variable with the bank's base rate; a fixed-rate option for the
first 3 years is available at 12.25% p.a.

## Eligibility
Sri Lankan resident aged 18-60, with stable income (salaried, self-employed or professional) and a
monthly instalment not exceeding 40% of net income. The property must have clear, mortgageable title.

## Security and Fees
The financed property is mortgaged to ABC Bank. Costs include a 1.00% processing fee, legal fees,
valuation fee, and mortgage-bond stamp duty and registration. Property insurance and (optionally)
decreasing-term life insurance assigned to the bank are required.

## Required Documents
Application form; National Identity Card; proof of income (salary slips / financial statements);
copy of the title deed, survey plan, and street line / non-vesting certificates; and a sale
agreement or BOQ (bill of quantities) for construction.

## Foreign Currency Home Loan
For non-resident Sri Lankans and eligible foreign-currency earners, a home loan in a currency of
choice (USD, GBP, EUR, AUD). Illustrative rate 7.50% p.a.; repayment from foreign-currency income
or an inward remittance. See Non-Resident Banking (ABC-NR-001).

## Procedure
Pre-approval can be obtained based on income before selecting a property. After property selection,
the bank performs a legal title check and valuation, issues a letter of offer, and disburses in
stages for construction or in full for a purchase, after the mortgage bond is executed.
