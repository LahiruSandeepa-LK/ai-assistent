# Overview
ABC Bank (fictional) offers unsecured and secured personal loans for salaried employees,
professionals and pensioners. Rates below are illustrative and subject to change.

## Personal Loan (Unsecured)
For permanent, salaried employees. Loan amount: LKR 100,000 to LKR 5,000,000. Tenure: 1 to 7
years. Illustrative interest rate: 13.50% p.a. to 16.00% p.a. (reducing balance), based on income
and credit profile. A processing fee of 1.00% of the loan amount (minimum LKR 5,000) applies.

## Eligibility
Applicant must be a Sri Lankan resident aged 18-60 at application and below 60 at loan maturity,
with a minimum monthly net income of LKR 50,000 and at least 6 months of continuous employment.
The total monthly instalment generally may not exceed 40% of net monthly income (debt-to-income
ratio).

## Required Documents
Completed application form; copy of National Identity Card; last 3 months' salary slips; last 6
months' bank statements; a salary-confirmation / employment letter from the employer. Professionals
may substitute proof of professional registration and income tax returns.

## Professionals' Loan
Concessionary loan for doctors, engineers, accountants, lawyers and other registered
professionals. Amounts up to LKR 10,000,000 at an illustrative 12.50% p.a., with tenure up to 7
years and reduced documentation.

## Educational Loan
Finances local and foreign higher-education costs (tuition, living expenses). Amounts up to LKR
5,000,000, tenure up to 8 years, illustrative rate 11.50% p.a. A guarantor or parental co-applicant
is usually required.

## Pensioners' ('Yasasa') and Pension Advance Loans
For government and approved private-sector pensioners up to age 70 at maturity. Illustrative rate
13.00% p.a., amount based on monthly pension, with repayment by standing order against the pension.

## Procedure
Submit the application and documents at any branch or via digital banking. The branch verifies
income and KYC, a credit assessment and CRIB (credit bureau) check is performed, and on approval
the loan is disbursed to the applicant's ABC Bank account, typically within 3-5 working days.
